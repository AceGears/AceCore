# AceCore privacy

AceCore collects no personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, and it makes no network requests of its own.

- **Files:** AceCore reads the folders you choose through Android's folder picker. Optionally, you can allow Android's "All files access" so RPG Maker XP/VX/VX Ace games run directly from their folders instead of from a copy; AceCore then still only opens the game folders in your library. AceCore never deletes or modifies your game files. The games themselves may write their own save files into their folder, exactly as on a PC, and AceCore writes a save into your game folder only when you ask it to (copying saves out of a private copy, or restoring a save backup). Removing a game from the library only removes AceCore's own entry.
- **Stored data:** your library, categories, play statistics, settings, custom covers, cheat profiles, save backups, file-index caches and web-game saves are stored privately on your device. Uninstalling AceCore or clearing its data removes them.
- **Network:** games run offline. A game can reach the internet only if you turn on "Allow internet access" (globally or for that game).
- **Diagnostics:** the diagnostic report is generated only when you tap "Copy diagnostic report", and goes only to your clipboard.
