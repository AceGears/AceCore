# AceCore privacy

AceCore collects no personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, and it makes no network requests of its own.

- **Files:** AceCore only reads folders you choose through Android's folder picker. It never asks for all-files access. It never modifies or deletes your game files. Removing a game from the library only removes AceCore's own entry.
- **Stored data:** your library, settings, custom covers, file-index caches and web-game saves are stored privately on your device. Uninstalling AceCore or clearing its data removes them.
- **Network:** games run offline. A game can reach the internet only if you turn on "Allow internet access" (globally or for that game).
- **Diagnostics:** the diagnostic report is generated only when you tap "Copy diagnostic report", and goes only to your clipboard.
